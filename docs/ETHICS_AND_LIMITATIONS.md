# Ethics, risks, and limitations

- Prices may be delayed, corrected, duplicated, or inconsistently reported.
- A missing mandi record does not mean no trading occurred.
- Wholesale prices do not equal a farmer's realised farm-gate price.
- Transport cost, spoilage, fees, quality, quantity, and access constraints affect actual decisions.
- Weather and satellite joins can introduce spatial and temporal mismatch.
- Rare shocks caused by policy, export restrictions, disease, conflict, or extreme weather may be outside training experience.
- Recommendations may favour markets with better reporting and disadvantage poorly measured regions.
- Probability is not certainty, and an interval is not a guarantee for every individual forecast.
- Causal modules require exchangeability, overlap, consistency, and correct temporal ordering.
- Personal or farm-level data must not be collected without purpose, consent, minimisation, and retention controls.

Before production use, perform domain-expert review, user research, external validation, security testing, accessibility review, monitoring ownership, a rollback plan, and a clear human-override process.
